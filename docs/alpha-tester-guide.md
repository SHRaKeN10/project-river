# Project River — closed alpha tester guide

Thanks for helping test. This is **play money only** — there's nothing real to
win or lose, and chips are free to top up.

## Get the app

1. Install **Expo Go** from the App Store / Play Store.
2. Open the link the organiser sends you (or scan their QR code). Project River
   loads inside Expo Go.
3. Tap **Create account** — email, a username, a passphrase. You start with
   10,000 chips.

> If a screen ever gets stuck, fully close Expo Go and reopen the link. Your
> seat and chips are safe on the server.

## What to try

- **Join a table** from the lobby. Tap an open seat, pick a buy-in, sit down.
- **Play hands** — fold / check / call / bet / raise / all-in. Play a few
  orbits so the button moves around.
- **Leave and come back** — leave the table, rejoin, check your chip balance
  looks right.
- **Drop your connection** mid-hand (airplane mode for a few seconds, then
  back). You should reconnect to your seat.
- **Run out of chips** — bust a stack, then use **Get free chips** to top up.
- **Multi-table** — have two of you sit at the same table and actually play
  each other.

## Reporting a problem

Tell the organiser:

- **What you expected vs what happened**, and roughly when.
- If a **pot looked wrong**: the table name and, if you can, the hand — every
  finished hand has an id (the organiser can pull the exact replay from it).
- If the app **froze or logged you out**: what you were doing, and whether
  reopening fixed it.

Screenshots help. "It felt slow" is a valid report — say which screen.

## Known rough edges (already on the list)

- No on-device polish pass yet — layout may be tight on small phones.
- If you're removed for being away too long, the app won't say why — you'll
  just be back in the lobby. Your chips are returned.
- Hand replays (organiser-side) show every player's cards, including folded
  hands.
